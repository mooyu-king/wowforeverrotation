WoW Forever Class Tier List: Level 60 DPS

November 6, 2026 snapshot · Single raid boss · With trinkets

Survival Hunter leads at 1,288 simulated DPS with a build that moves into melee between shots. Its full-melee alternative scores 954. If you need to stay at range, compare the turret Hunter rows before picking a class.

Encounter and loadout

Level 60 characters attack one stationary level 63 boss for 120 seconds, with a 15-second variation. Each preset uses its own race, talents, gear and two trinkets. Raid buffs, target debuffs and consumables are included; world buffs are excluded. MythicSim ran 10,000 iterations on its reference characters. The ranking page associates this sweep with the November 2 development notes, client 70707 and engine revision a667dce.

DPS by specialization and playstyle

All 28 damage builds in the source snapshot are listed, including alternate Hunter and Warlock playstyles and four experimental hybrids. Percentages compare each row to the leading build. How the grades work

Divide each displayed DPS result by 1,288. Our editorial bands use the unrounded ratio: S at 90% or higher, A from 80% to below 90%, B from 70% to below 80%, and C below 70%. For example, Retribution reaches 85.2% and Enhancement 83.3%, so both earn A. Shockadin reaches 79.8% and lands in B despite rounding to 80% on a whole-percent display. These bands group reference results; a small gap near a cutoff can disappear with a gear or encounter change.

Limits and sources

These are simulations. Extra targets, downtime, mechanics, group composition and player execution can change the outcome. Survival weaving assumes the modeled travel timing works in a raid. Shockadin, Elehance, Frostfire and Smite use experimental models. The full-DPS table spans all nine classes; dedicated tank and healing builds need role-specific measures. Leveling speed, PvP effectiveness and live raid performance require separate evidence.
