COMBAT GUIDE

Opening sequence: your first moves

Level 60 · ranged Beast Mastery · active pet, Summon Hawk and Bestial Wrath · one durable target

  1. Mark the assigned enemy before the pull. Position where you can stand still as Auto Shot becomes ready.
  2. On the pull, send your pet to the marked target and begin ranged attacks. Check that the pet has reached melee range.
  3. Apply Serpent Sting, establish hawk uptime with Summon Hawk, and fit Aimed Shot into your ranged cycle.

What to use next

  • Maintain Sting on a target that will live through its ticks. Use Summon Hawk as available to keep two hawks attacking.
  • Time movement between Auto Shots and keep your pet connected on target changes. A ready button can wait if a shot is about to fire.
  • Use Bestial Wrath when your pet can stay on the enemy. If the boss is about to leave, wait until the pet has a reliable attack window.

Beast Mastery Hunter: understand the next action

The guide’s ranged priorities and the practice spell timers serve different purposes. The guide helps select the next useful action; the timers explain when an ability is available. This practice has an active pet, a reachable stationary target and unmodified spell costs. It does not supply an invented weapon speed or automatically award Auto Shots.

What to watch

Pet connection, target effects and separate cooldowns.

2 Pet Attack

Command reference ↗

A pet command, not a player spell. Reissuing is permitted. The exercise assumes the pet can reach the selected target.

3 Serpent Sting

Spell #25295 ↗

Rank 9 maintains a 15-second target effect. This profile uses the base 250 Mana cost.

4 Summon Hawk

Spell #1293241 ↗

Summons an 18-second hawk; at most two are active. Its cooldown is shared with Arcane Shot. Refresh behavior at the cap is not modeled.

5 Aimed Shot

Spell #20904 ↗

Rank 6 has a 2-second cast and a 6-second cooldown, shared with Multi-Shot. Those other shots and Auto Shot timing are outside this exercise.

6 Bestial Wrath

Spell #19574 ↗

Requires an active pet. A connected attacking pet is a recommendation, not a cast restriction. Base cooldown 120 seconds; duration 18 seconds.

Try an experiment

Use Bestial Wrath early in a run, then inspect it again. Its cooldown remains 120 seconds even though a session lasts one minute. Use Aimed Shot and watch the two-second cast before its cooldown begins. Cancel a cast to see that the effect does not occur. In a second run, use the separate target-removal control and reconnect your pet.

The walkthrough supplies prepared states for each checkpoint and does not run a combat clock. Continuous practice uses the stated spell cooldowns and cast times. A one-minute session is a session length, not a speed multiplier. A legal action remains available even when it is not the coach’s preferred choice.

The right-hand guide can follow the coach or stay on a selected skill. On mobile it moves into the practice area. Reading a skill does not pause time; use Pause when you want to study the explanation. Test without hints keeps the same rules and removes the recommendation highlight.

Common mistakes and recovery

Bestial Wrath does not become ready again

The 60-second session does not shorten the source’s 120-second cooldown. Start a fresh session to rehearse another opening.

The pet has not been sent to the target

An active pet is required for Bestial Wrath. Having it attacking is a good timing decision; it is not an additional restriction imposed by this tool.

Two hawks are already active

Their independent expiry timers continue running. Further summons at the cap are marked unevaluated until the replacement behavior is verified, rather than asserted to be forbidden in game.

Frequently asked questions

Does this measure shot clipping?

No. Auto Shot timing, weapon speed, haste and movement are not modeled. The old fixed three-second shot counter has been removed.

Why are there no automatic target swaps?

The old 20- and 40-second swaps were exercise events, not skill rules. A separate, labeled scenario control now lets you practice losing a target when you choose.

Is the mana pool my level-60 value?

No. The editable starting and base mana are scenario inputs. Replace them with your character’s values; base mana is not your full mana after Intellect.
