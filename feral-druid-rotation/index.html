COMBAT GUIDE

Opening sequence: your first moves

Level 60 · Cat Form DPS · Shifting Power · sustained single target vulnerable to bleeds

  1. Take Cat Form and reach the target's rear when positioning permits. Open with Rake to start the short bleed.
  2. Use Shred from behind to build combo points. Watch your energy and Rake timer between casts.
  3. Spend the first full set of available points on Rip while the target has time to take its damage.

What to use next

  • Refresh Rake and Rip as they run out. Budget energy and points for the next Rip before committing to an extra finisher.
  • Use Shifting Power when the energy gain will fit. Each use draws from your mana pool, so spread uses over the fight.
  • With both bleeds healthy, build again. Ferocious Bite spends spare points when you can still rebuild before Rip expires.

Feral Druid: understand the next action

The full Cat rotation guide above describes priority choices. The practice uses base spell costs, with Predatory Discipline enabled because the official talent update makes it a prerequisite for Shifting Power. Other cost modifiers and set bonuses are excluded. A talent can change the energy result even when the spell name stays the same.

What to watch

Energy, combo points and the two bleed timers.

Source-linked action reference

Keys 1–5 and touch use the same rules. Before starting, inspect an action to read its conditions without spending resources. Each reference opens in a new tab.

1 Rake

Spell #9904 ↗

Applies a 9-second bleed and awards one combo point. The displayed cost is the base Rank 4 cost, before talents.

2 Shred

Spell #9830 ↗

Requires the target’s rear. Awards one point. Base Rank 5 cost is 60 Energy; Shredding Attacks is not selected in this base-cost profile.

3 Rip

Spell #9896 ↗

Consumes 1–5 points. The guide recommends five, but fewer points are legal. Rank 6 lasts 12 seconds before modifiers.

4 Ferocious Bite

Spell #31018 ↗

Consumes 1–5 points and 35 Energy. This profile includes Predatory Discipline, the official prerequisite for Shifting Power, so extra Energy is preserved.

5 Shifting Power

Spell #1322605 ↗

Restores 40 Energy for 55% of base mana. Unavailable at full Energy under the official update. Above 50 but below 100 it remains legal; avoid wasting recovery.

In continuous practice, build a point and use Rip before reaching five: it is allowed, while the coach can explain why it prefers more points. Common mistakes and recovery

A finisher is available before five points

Availability and priority are different. The spell accepts at least one point; the guide recommends planning a stronger finisher.

Energy no longer refills automatically

Open Character inputs and enter the recovery rate you want to test. The default is zero because this tool does not infer your character’s resource behavior. Reset scenario energy is a separate control, not a spell.

Shifting Power is blocked at full Energy

This restriction is explicitly stated in the official update. It is different from the guide’s recommendation to use the skill around 50 Energy or below.

Spell profile and evidence

Version note: the earlier official preview described a 16-second Shifting Power cooldown reduced by talents. The linked later database entry lists 8 seconds; this profile uses that snapshot. Official updates also prohibit use at full Energy and make Predatory Discipline a prerequisite; both rules are applied here.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Pool Energy?

It has been removed from the spell bar. Waiting is not a game ability. The independent scenario controls let you reset a state without pretending to cast a spell.

Why is Shred more expensive than on my character?

The profile uses the source’s base Rank 5 cost. Cost-reducing talents and equipment are excluded and listed as limitations before you start.

Does this include Bear tanking?

No. Cat Form and rear positioning are assumed. Threat, mitigation, movement and Bear abilities are outside this profile.
