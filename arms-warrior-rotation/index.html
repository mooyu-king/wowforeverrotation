COMBAT GUIDE

Opening sequence: your first moves

Level 60 · two-handed Arms · Mortal Strike and 2/2 Bloodthrill · one bleedable target

  1. Set Battle Stance before the pull. Charge when your group's positioning permits it.
  2. Apply Rend early against a durable target that can bleed.
  3. Main-hand hits against a target carrying your Rend have a 20% chance at 2/2 Bloodthrill to open a six-second Overpower window. Watch for that opening while keeping enough rage to use Mortal Strike.

What to use next

  • Act on an available Overpower in Battle Stance. A dodge can open the attack; Bloodthrill also gives Rend a role.
  • Keep Mortal Strike available by budgeting rage before queueing extra attacks.
  • Use Heroic Strike as a surplus-rage outlet. Spending the next swing on it can leave too little rage for a higher-priority button.

In the drill, apply Rend and watch for Bloodthrill before using Overpower. Practice this opener
