FROM THE DEVELOPER NOTES

Rotation updates

Retribution Paladin

The November notes describe Unsealed Wrath storing a seal for 15 seconds, up to three stacks. An attack landing with a different seal releases them. Vengeance has a separate five-stack limit.

Blizzard notes Read guide notes
Arms Warrior

Forever's Arms play connects Rend and Overpower through Bloodthrill. The Battle Stance requirement matters when returning from a stance change.

Blizzard notes Read guide notes
Frost Mage

The November rework makes Cone of Cold the spender for Winter's Chill. Ice Lance empowered by Fingers of Frost contributes to the setup; Fire Blast and Ice Lance share a cooldown.

Blizzard notes Read guide notes
Combat Rogue

Poised is a Forever passive learned at level 18. Reaching maximum energy out of combat prepares a 10-energy discount on the next energy ability.
