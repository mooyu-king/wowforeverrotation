COMBAT GUIDE

Opening sequence: your first moves

Level 60 · Enhancement melee DPS · Stormstrike and Maelstrom Weapon · single-target PvE

  1. Prepare your weapon imbue and the group's assigned totems. Place the fire totem where it can reach the fight without hitting controlled enemies.
  2. Enter melee, start swinging and establish Flame Shock on the target. Use Stormstrike when it becomes available.
  3. Once you reach five Maelstrom Weapon stacks, spend them on Lightning Bolt. Until then, work around swings and available shocks.

What to use next

  • Track Stormstrike's cooldown and keep the target within melee reach so weapon attacks continue.
  • Maintain Flame Shock on a durable enemy and Magma Totem where hitting nearby enemies is safe. Between refreshes, Earth Shock is an option when you can afford its mana and cooldown.
  • At five Maelstrom stacks, fit Lightning Bolt into the next spell window. Keep totem placement in mind whenever a tank moves the fight.

Enhancement Shaman: understand the next action

Maelstrom is a chance-based result of melee attacks, not a timer that guarantees a charge. The tool does not guess the proc rate. You can supply a successful melee-proc event using a separate scenario control, then test how the resulting stack count changes Lightning Bolt. The referenced full-rank effect reduces both its cast time and mana cost.

What to watch

Totem uptime, shock recovery and actual Maelstrom state.

Try an experiment

Cast Lightning Bolt with zero stacks and observe its normal cast. Repeat with fewer than five charges to inspect the partial reduction. At five charges it becomes instant and free. No checkpoint should tell you that casting below five is impossible. Then compare Earth Shock and Flame Shock to observe their shared recovery window.

Common mistakes and recovery

Waiting does not grant Maelstrom stacks

Correct: time passing alone is not the trigger. Use the labeled melee-proc event to supply a situation. The control makes no claim about the chance of such an event.

Lightning Bolt is available below five stacks

It remains castable if its ordinary conditions are met. The coach’s recommendation to wait for five is distinct from cast legality.

Fire Nova is unavailable without a fire totem

The referenced ability explodes around an active fire totem. Prepare Magma Totem first in this profile.

Frequently asked questions

Does Stormstrike guarantee a stack?

No. The practice no longer adds a fixed charge on a timer or treats Stormstrike as a guaranteed full-stack button.

Can the tool predict proc frequency?

No. The source describes a chance on melee attacks; an exact live proc model has not been verified here. Scenario events are supplied explicitly.

Are damage and raid buffs calculated?

No. Stormstrike’s effect timer and consumption are tracked, but weapon damage, attack outcomes, group buffs, area damage and DPS are not calculated.
