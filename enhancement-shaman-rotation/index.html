Class guides / Enhancement Shaman
LEVEL 60 · SINGLE-TARGET PVE

Enhancement Shaman Rotation

Keep melee contact and turn Maelstrom stacks into a spell.

Opening sequence

Level 60 · Enhancement melee DPS · Stormstrike and Maelstrom Weapon · single-target PvE

  1. Prepare your weapon imbue and the group's assigned totems. Place the fire totem where it can reach the fight without hitting controlled enemies.
  2. Enter melee, start swinging and establish Flame Shock on the target. Use Stormstrike when it becomes available.
  3. Once you reach five Maelstrom Weapon stacks, spend them on Lightning Bolt. Until then, work around swings and available shocks.

What to use next

  • Track Stormstrike's cooldown and keep the target within melee reach so weapon attacks continue.
  • Maintain Flame Shock on a durable enemy and Magma Totem where hitting nearby enemies is safe. Between refreshes, Earth Shock is an option when you can afford its mana and cooldown.
  • At five Maelstrom stacks, fit Lightning Bolt into the next spell window. Keep totem placement in mind whenever a tank moves the fight.

Common mistake

Lightning Bolt follows a stack condition. Casting it after every Stormstrike regardless of stacks can cost you time in melee.

Forever class changes

The November class-development notes are the reference point for Enhancement's launch talents. The full-stack Lightning Bolt described here assumes a Maelstrom Weapon build.
