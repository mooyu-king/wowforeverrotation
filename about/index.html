About Us

Independent guides for Forever

WoW Forever Rotation is a fan-made learning resource for World of Warcraft: Forever. Start with a specialization, check the equipment and talents used, then try a 60-second training fight. Press a skill key or tap its button and watch how the target and your resources change. The untimed questions below the fight let you review one decision at a time.

How we use sources

Blizzard development notes and hotfixes describe game changes. Linked class guides add context for openers and priorities. Practice feedback applies to the setup shown on the page; it is not a prediction of your damage in game. See the sources on each guide to check a rule or compare builds.

Corrections

Send us the specialization, the affected step and a link to the relevant notes. We review the guide and its practice answers together. This independent site is unaffiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
