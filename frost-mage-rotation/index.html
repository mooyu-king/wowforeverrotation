COMBAT GUIDE

Opening sequence: your first moves

Level 60 · 5/5 Winter's Chill and 2/2 Fingers of Frost · single target · room to position for Cone of Cold

  1. Begin with Frostbolt from a safe position.
  2. Watch the target's Winter's Chill counter as qualifying spells land.
  3. Use an empowered Ice Lance when the opportunity appears. Set up a safe angle and range for Cone of Cold at five stacks.

What to use next

  • Track stacks on the intended target, especially after switching enemies.
  • Spend a full stack with Cone of Cold when you can actually hit. Avoid crossing a dangerous area solely to reach the target.
  • Return to Frostbolt between proc and cooldown decisions. Check your shared cooldown before committing Fire Blast.
PATCH NOTES

What changed

The November rework makes Cone of Cold the spender for Winter's Chill. Ice Lance empowered by Fingers of Frost contributes to the setup; Fire Blast and Ice Lance share a cooldown.
