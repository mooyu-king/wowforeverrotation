Compare four WoW Forever rotations

Choose two level 60 damage specializations. Compare the decisions you make against one target, then try their openers.

Retribution Paladin

What to watch
Seal stacks and swing timing
Key decision
A landed swing under a different seal spends the stored charges.
Common mistake
Switching the seal twice before a swing connects.
How to recover
Watch the next hit before switching back.

Arms Warrior

What to watch
Rage and short attack windows
Key decision
Rend and a main-hand attack can open a Bloodthrill window.
Common mistake
Spending rage before the next Mortal Strike.
How to recover
Save rage and check the remaining Overpower window.
Practice Arms Warrior →

Frost Mage

What to watch
Target stacks and spell charges
Key decision
Build Winter’s Chill and spend it with Cone of Cold in range.
Common mistake
Changing targets and expecting the stacks to follow.
How to recover
Check stacks on the current enemy before spending.
Practice Frost Mage →

Combat Rogue

What to watch
Energy, combo points and buff time
Key decision
Build points, keep Slice and Dice running, then spend spare points on damage.
Common mistake
Using a damage finisher just before Slice and Dice ends.
How to recover
Rebuild a point and restore the attack-speed buff.
